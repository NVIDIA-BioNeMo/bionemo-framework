# Research notes

Start from the biological decision. Prefer direct target-phage/target-strain evidence, then close relatives, then clearly labeled transfer evidence. Use primary papers and appropriate biological databases.

For methods or quantitative claims that are not common knowledge or already summarized locally, consult the relevant full paper or supplement. Follow Data Availability statements to dataset records and validate that a downloaded payload has the claimed biological format before using it.

Capture denominators, controls, sample size, assay conditions, uncertainty, negative results, and contradictions that affect the decision. For predictive models, note the training domain, validation population, operating point, and limits of transfer. When no defensible cutoff exists, propose target-specific calibration instead of inventing a universal threshold.

- [EMA draft phage-therapy quality guideline record](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/quality-aspects-phage-therapy-medicinal-products) and the recipe's local historical transcription.

Use the publisher or repository copy when full text or supplementary material is needed. Record the version consulted and follow its Data Availability links; there is no need to vendor a transformed copy into the recipe.
