# RL monitoring guidance

Run training through a facility whose worker, status, and logs survive the chat process. Retain a stable job identifier, observe startup and early progress, then check meaningful progress until the facility reports terminal success or failure. Reattach to a live job after a new session instead of launching a duplicate.

At each comparable validation event, report the fixed validation-bank size, component means and support/denominators, missing or invalid rates, hard-QC yield, raw and 99%-clustered diversity, copying, and uncertainty. A positive aggregate must not hide an inactive component.

Select checkpoints from sustained, meaningful validation improvement. Continue long enough to distinguish noise, temporary degradation, rebound, and collapse, within the approved ceiling. Preserve the latest resumable checkpoint and the best scientific checkpoint.
